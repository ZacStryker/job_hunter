# Privacy Policy for HITLOBSTER

**Last updated: June 16, 2026**

This Privacy Policy describes how HITLOBSTER ("HITLOBSTER", "we", "us", or "our")
collects, uses, stores, and protects your information when you use our
application (the "Service"). By using the Service, you agree to the practices
described in this policy.

If you have any questions, contact us at **admin@hitlobster.ai**.

---

## 1. Who We Are

HITLOBSTER is a personal job-application tracking dashboard. It helps you
organize your job search by collecting application-related information in one
place, including — with your explicit permission — relevant email correspondence
from your Gmail account.

The Service is operated by HITLOBSTER and hosted at **https://hitlobster.ai/**.

---

## 2. Information We Collect

### a. Account Information
When you create an account, we collect your email address and a securely hashed
password. We use this to authenticate you and to send account-related messages
(such as activation and password-reset links).

### b. Google Account Data
With your explicit consent, HITLOBSTER connects to your Google account using
Google OAuth 2.0 and requests the following scope:

- **`https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.metadata`** — read-only access to the
  metadata of your Gmail messages and labels. This scope does **not** grant
  access to the body or contents of any message.

We use this **read-only** access to:

- Display the Gmail labels in your account so you can choose which ones map to
  your job applications;
- Read **only the metadata** of messages within the labels you select — namely
  the **sender ("From") address, subject line, and received date/time** — in
  order to surface job-application correspondence (such as recruiter emails,
  interview invitations, and application confirmations) inside your HITLOBSTER
  dashboard.

HITLOBSTER **does not access, read, or store the body or contents of your email
messages**, attachments, or any data beyond the From, Subject, and Date metadata
described above. HITLOBSTER also **cannot** send, delete, modify, or compose
email. The access we request is strictly read-only.

### c. Information You Provide
Any job-application details, notes, company information, or other content you
manually enter into the dashboard.

## 3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect solely to:

- Provide, operate, and maintain the Service;
- Display the metadata (sender, subject, and date) of your job-application
  correspondence within your personal dashboard;
- Authenticate you and secure your account;
- Communicate with you about your account.

We do **not** use your Google user data for advertising, and we do **not** sell
your data.

## 4. Limited Use Disclosure (Google API Services User Data Policy)

HITLOBSTER's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to
the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy),
including the **Limited Use** requirements.

Specifically:

- We only use access to Google user data to provide and improve user-facing
  features that are prominent in HITLOBSTER's interface.
- We do **not** transfer or sell Google user data to third parties, except as
  necessary to provide or improve the Service, to comply with applicable law, or
  as part of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets with notice to users.
- We do **not** use Google user data for serving advertisements.
- We do **not** allow humans to read your Google user data unless: (a) we have
  your affirmative consent for specific messages; (b) it is necessary for
  security purposes (such as investigating abuse); (c) it is required to comply
  with applicable law; or (d) the data has been aggregated and anonymized and is
  used for internal operations.

## 5. How We Store and Protect Your Data

- Your data is stored in a private database on infrastructure we control and is
  not shared with other users.
- Connections to the Service are encrypted in transit using TLS (HTTPS).
- OAuth access and refresh tokens are stored securely and are used only to make
  authorized requests to the Google APIs on your behalf.
- We restrict access to your data to the minimum necessary to operate the
  Service.

No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we take reasonable
measures to protect your information.

---

## 6. Data Sharing

We do **not** sell, rent, or trade your personal information or Google user data.
We may share information only:

- With service providers strictly necessary to host and operate the Service,
  bound by confidentiality obligations;
- When required by law or valid legal process;
- To protect the rights, property, or safety of HITLOBSTER, our users, or the
  public.

---

## 7. Data Retention and Deletion

- We retain your data for as long as your account is active.
- You may disconnect Google access at any time from within HITLOBSTER, or by
  revoking access directly at
  [https://myaccount.google.com/permissions](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions).
  Revoking access stops all further data collection from your Google account.
- You may request deletion of your account and all associated data — including
  any data obtained from Google — by emailing **admin@hitlobster.ai**. We will
  delete your data within 30 days of a verified request.
